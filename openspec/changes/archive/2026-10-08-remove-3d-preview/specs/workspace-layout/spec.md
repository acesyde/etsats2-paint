## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: 3D preview panel placeholder
**Reason**: The 3D preview was never more than a placeholder, and the product dropped it: vehicle models won't be rendered.
**Migration**: None. The central area always shows the canvas, and the shortcuts Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+1, 2 and 3 are free.

### Requirement: View modes
**Reason**: With no 3D preview, the only remaining mode is the canvas. The 2D / 3D / Split control, the View menu items and their shortcuts have nothing left to switch.
**Migration**: None. Projects always open showing their canvas, and a view mode remembered in the preferences is ignored.
