When no project is open, TruckPaint shows its home screen. It is where you start a new livery, open one you saved, or pick up recent work.

## The home screen

The home screen holds:

- **<Ui id="home-new-project" />**, the main button, which starts a new project (Ctrl+N, ⌘ on macOS);
- **<Ui id="cmd-open-project" />**, which opens a `.truckpaint` file you saved (Ctrl+O);
- **<Ui id="home-recent" />**, one card per project you opened, the most recent first;
- **<Ui id="home-preferences" />**, at the top right.

<Shot name="getting-started/home" alt="The TruckPaint home screen, with the New Project and Open Project buttons and a recent project card" />

The first time, the recent projects area is empty and offers <Ui id="home-recent-empty-create" />, which does the same as <Ui id="home-new-project" />. To create your first project now, go to [Create your first project](../first-project/).

## Reopen recent work

Each recent project card shows a thumbnail of the project's first main texture, its name, where its file is and when you last opened it. Click a card to open the project.

When a project's file was moved or deleted, its card is marked as unavailable and can't be opened. Its remove button (<Ui id="home-remove-recent" /> on hover) takes it off the list.

:::note[After a crash]
While a project has unsaved changes, TruckPaint keeps a recovery copy of it every two minutes, without touching your own file. If the app closes unexpectedly, the next launch shows <Ui id="home-recovered" /> above the recent projects. Choose <Ui id="home-restore" /> to get your work back, or <Ui id="home-discard" /> to throw the copy away.
:::

## Choose your language

TruckPaint speaks English, French, Spanish and German. At first it follows your system's language, and falls back to English for any other language.

To pick another one:

1. Open the preferences: <Ui id="home-preferences" /> on the home screen, <Ui id="menu-edit" /> › <Ui id="cmd-preferences" /> on Windows and Linux, or <Ui id="menu-app" /> › <Ui id="app-menu-settings" /> on macOS (Ctrl+,).
2. Under <Ui id="prefs-language" />, choose a language. The whole interface switches at once, without a restart.

Choose <Ui id="language-system" /> to follow your system's language again.

## Adjust the interface

The preferences also hold:

- <Ui id="prefs-ui-scale" /> and <Ui id="prefs-text-size" />, to make the interface and its text larger or smaller, with a live preview. <Ui id="prefs-reset" /> brings them back to 100%.
- <Ui id="prefs-template-packs" />, the folders of the template packs used to install the official vehicles, one per game. You set them the first time you pick an official vehicle: see [Official vehicles](../../vehicles/official-vehicles/).
- On Windows and Linux, <Ui id="prefs-system-title-bar" />.

TruckPaint remembers your preferences, the size and place of its window, and your recent projects between sessions.
