The tools draw vector shapes on the active texture. Sizes and positions are in texture pixels, so a 512 px band is 512 pixels of the exported texture, whatever the zoom. Hover a tool in the tool rail to see its name and shortcut.

New shapes take the fill and stroke shown in the inspector when nothing is selected (see [Color and gradients](../color-and-gradients/)), and go at the top of the active layer.

## Draw rectangles and ellipses

1. Pick the <Ui id="tool-rectangle" /> or <Ui id="tool-ellipse" /> tool.
2. Drag on the texture. Hold Shift for a square or a circle, and Alt (Option on macOS) to draw from the center.
3. Release. The shape is selected and the tool stays active for the next one.

Press Escape during the drag to cancel. Round the corners of a rectangle with <Ui id="props-corner-radius" /> in the inspector's <Ui id="inspector-appearance" /> section.

## Draw polygons and stars

With the <Ui id="tool-polygon" /> tool, drag a box: the polygon fills it, first point up. Shift keeps it regular. Set <Ui id="props-sides" /> (3 to 12), <Ui id="props-star" /> and <Ui id="props-inner-radius" /> in the tool options bar above the canvas, before you draw. When polygons are selected, the same settings appear in the inspector's <Ui id="inspector-polygon" /> section and change them in place.

## Draw paths with the Pen

1. Pick the <Ui id="tool-pen" /> tool.
2. Click to add corner points. Press and drag to add a smooth point with curve handles. Shift keeps the new point and handles at 45° steps.
3. Click the first point to close the shape, or press Enter or Escape to leave the path open.

Backspace, or Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on macOS), removes the last point while you draw.

<Clip name="workshop/draw-path" label="Clicking four points with the Pen tool and clicking the first one to close the shape" />

## Draw lines

With the <Ui id="tool-line" /> tool, drag from one end to the other; Shift snaps the angle to 45° steps and Alt draws from the middle. Lines and open paths are painted with the fill color. Their thickness is the <Ui id="field-width" /> field of the inspector (8 px at first); click it to set <Ui id="line-dashed" />, <Ui id="line-cap" /> and <Ui id="line-join" />.

## Select, move, resize and rotate

With the <Ui id="tool-selection" /> tool:

- **Click** an object to select it; a click on an object inside a group selects the group, and Ctrl-click selects the object itself. **Shift-click** adds or removes. **Drag** on empty canvas to select with a marquee. <Ui id="cmd-select-all" /> is Ctrl+A, Escape deselects.
- **Drag** the selection to move it; Shift keeps the move straight or at 45°. The arrow keys nudge by 1 px, by 10 px with Shift.
- **Drag a handle** to resize: Shift keeps the proportions, Alt resizes from the center.
- **Drag just outside a corner** to rotate; Shift snaps to 15° steps.

<Clip name="workshop/move-resize" label="Clicking the ellipse, dragging it, then dragging a corner handle" />

For exact values, type them in the inspector's <Ui id="inspector-layout" /> section: <Ui id="transform-x" />, <Ui id="transform-y" />, <Ui id="field-width" />, <Ui id="field-height" />, <Ui id="transform-rotation" /> and <Ui id="transform-scale" />. You can also drag on a field's label to change its value.

<Ui id="cmd-duplicate" /> (Ctrl+D), <Ui id="cmd-copy" />, <Ui id="cmd-paste" /> and <Ui id="cmd-delete" /> are in the <Ui id="menu-edit" /> menu. Copied objects can be pasted on another texture or into another project.

## Edit points

The <Ui id="tool-direct-selection" /> tool shows a path's points. Click a point (Shift-click for more) and drag it, or drag its handles; Alt-drag a handle to break a smooth point into a corner. Double-click a point to switch it between corner and smooth, double-click a segment to add a point, and press Delete to remove the selected points.

Rectangles, ellipses and polygons have no editable points until you choose <Ui id="menu-object" /> › <Ui id="cmd-convert-to-path" />.

## Line things up

- **Snapping** pulls what you move or draw onto the grid, guides, the texture's edges and center, and other objects' edges and centers. Toggle it with <Ui id="menu-view" /> › <Ui id="cmd-snapping" /> or in the status bar; hold Ctrl during a drag to ignore it.
- **Guides:** drag from a ruler onto the canvas. Drag a guide back onto a ruler to delete it. <Ui id="cmd-show-guides" /> and <Ui id="cmd-clear-guides" /> are in the <Ui id="menu-view" /> menu.
- **Grid:** <Ui id="menu-view" /> › <Ui id="cmd-show-grid" />. Set its <Ui id="prefs-grid-spacing" /> in <Ui id="cmd-preferences" /> (64 px by default).

Guides and the grid are never exported.

## Align and distribute

Select objects and use the buttons of the <Ui id="inspector-layout" /> section, or <Ui id="menu-object" /> › <Ui id="menu-align" />. <Ui id="transform-align-to" /> picks the reference: <Ui id="align-to-selection" />, <Ui id="align-to-artboard" /> (the whole texture, handy to center a logo), or <Ui id="align-to-key-object" /> (the last object you selected). Distributing needs three objects or more.

## Combine shapes

Select two shapes or more and choose <Ui id="menu-object" /> › <Ui id="menu-combine" />:

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| <Ui id="op-unite" /> (Ctrl+Shift+U) | one shape covering them all |
| <Ui id="op-minus-front" /> (Ctrl+Shift+-) | the bottom shape, cut by the shapes above it |
| <Ui id="op-intersect" /> | only where they all overlap |
| <Ui id="op-exclude" /> | where they don't overlap |

The result is one path. Texts must go through <Ui id="cmd-create-outlines" /> first; images and open lines can't be combined.

## Flip

<Ui id="op-flip-horizontal" /> (Shift+H) and <Ui id="op-flip-vertical" /> (Shift+V), in the <Ui id="menu-object" /> menu and the inspector, mirror the selection in place.

## Outlines

The <Ui id="panel-stroke" /> row of the inspector adds an outline to shapes and texts, inside, outside or centered on the edge. See [Color and gradients](../color-and-gradients/).
